Question 3:
In this assignment scenario, which party is acting according to the principle of maximum expected utililty? The customer? Your program? Both?

Customers act according to the principle of maximum utility since they choose between the expected utility of being uninsured
and the expected utility of being insured. Whichever expected utility ends up being higher is the option they want to choose.
The program also acts according to the principle of maximum utility, where its utility can be infered to be U($) = expected profit.
It sorts customers from highest expected profit to lowest expected profit, choosing to take whichever customers are the highest while the budget allows.
